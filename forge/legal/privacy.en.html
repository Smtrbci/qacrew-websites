5. International transfers

Because the servers of the providers above are located outside Türkiye, your data is transferred abroad. These transfers are made under Article 9 of the KVKK, on the basis of the standard contracts published by the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board and other appropriate safeguards.

6. AI processing

The text you enter in Forge is sent to Anthropic via the Claude API to generate documents. Under our terms with this provider, your data is not used to train its models. We do not use your content to train AI models either.

7. How we collect data

We collect data electronically, by automated or partly automated means, through the sign-up form, your use of the application, the Paddle checkout, cookies and support correspondence.

8. Cookies

Forge uses essential cookies and local storage to keep you signed in, keep the Service secure and remember your preferences (language, light/dark mode). Visitor statistics are collected with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not use cookies. Non-essential analytics or marketing cookies are used only with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

9. Retention

Data Retention period

Invoices and transaction records As required by tax and commercial law (generally 10 years)
Security and access logs Up to 2 years
Marketing consent records Until consent is withdrawn, then for the period required by law

At the end of these periods your data is deleted, destroyed or anonymised.

10. Security

We protect your data with technical and organisational measures such as encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), password hashing, access control and server-side quota and permission checks. No transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

11. Your rights

Under Article 11 of the KVKK (and, where applicable, the GDPR) you have the right to:

  • learn whether your personal data is processed and request information about it;
  • learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used accordingly;
  • know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom it is transferred;
  • request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data;
  • request deletion or destruction under the conditions of Article 7 of the KVKK;
  • request that corrections and deletions be notified to the third parties it was transferred to;
  • object to a result against you arising solely from automated analysis;
  • claim compensation if you suffer damage from unlawful processing.

Send your request from your registered email address to info@qacrew.it, or in writing to İslambey Mah. Akansel Sk., Arnavutköy/Istanbul, Türkiye. Requests are answered free of charge within 30 days at the latest. If you are not satisfied with the outcome, you may complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board, or to your local data protection authority if you are in the EEA.

12. Children

Forge is not intended for people under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

13. Changes

We may update this policy. We will announce material changes by email or in-app notice; the current version is always available on this page.
