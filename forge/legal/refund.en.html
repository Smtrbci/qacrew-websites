Refund Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

If you are not satisfied with your Forge subscription, you can request a full refund within 14 days of your first payment or any renewal payment, no questions asked.

  • How to request: Use the link in the receipt email from Paddle, go to paddle.net, or email us at info@qacrew.it.
  • Who processes it: Our order process is conducted by our online reseller Paddle.com. Paddle.com is the Merchant of Record for all our orders and processes refunds.
  • Where the money goes: Back to your original payment method. Depending on your bank, it may take 5–10 business days to appear.
  • Free trial: If you cancel during the 7-day trial, you are not charged at all.
  • After a refund: Once the refund is approved, the related subscription is cancelled and plan access ends.
  • After 14 days: If you cancel, your subscription stays active until the end of the current period; no partial refunds are given for the remaining time. This does not limit your statutory rights.

Paddle may decline a refund request if it detects fraud or refund abuse. Paddle's buyer terms and refund policy also apply.
